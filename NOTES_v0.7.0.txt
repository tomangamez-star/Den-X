DenX Animator v0.7.0 — Coordinate In-betweening

- Deterministic 25%, 50%, 75%, and custom coordinate tween generation.
- Interpolates matching figure nodes, style scale, segment lengths, camera transforms, text transforms, colors, and drawing layers.
- Inserts the generated pose after the current frame and shifts the former next frame forward.
- Tween insertion participates in timeline undo/redo, including text state.
- Removes the duplicate v0.6 frame-change restoration that could hide figures while navigating backward.
- Moves DenX Assistant away from the bottom-right zoom controls.
- Adds permanent release signing support for Palm Store and future in-place Android updates.
