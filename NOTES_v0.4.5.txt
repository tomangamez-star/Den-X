DenX Animator v0.4.5 — Toolbox Rebuild / No-Scroll Engine

This patch stops trying to repair the old scrolling toolbar.

What changed
- Left toolbox is rebuilt as a fixed page-based control panel.
- There is NO vertical scroll surface in the left toolbox anymore.
- The compact quick rail switches Project / General / Draw / Animate / Figure / Camera pages.
- Canvas/renderer and right contextual panels live in their own fixed stage region.
- v0.4.4 Copy/Paste Figure and MP4 Export Studio remain untouched.
- Right contextual rails no longer inherit left-toolbar sizing behavior.
- Whole-figure glow remains disabled; selected/neighbor node states stay lightweight.
- Visible app footer updated to Version 0.4.5.

Why
The repeated corruption was strongly tied to Android Chromium repainting the complex left
scroll surface. v0.4.5 removes that scroll path entirely instead of adding more compositor CSS.

Test
1. Tap every quick rail page repeatedly.
2. Spam Pan / Select / Draw / Camera.
3. Select/deselect figures repeatedly.
4. Pan and zoom the stage.
5. Leave the editor open and switch pages for 30–60 seconds.
6. Confirm whether stale/duplicated toolbar tiles ever appear.
